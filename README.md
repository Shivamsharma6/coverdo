# Coverdo

> Operational execution partner for Indian D2C and ecommerce brands. Owning the outcome between your store, couriers, and messaging.

Coverdo takes over the operational leaks ecommerce brands bleed on — delivery exceptions (NDR), dubious COD orders, cart objections, and broken contribution reporting. Standalone scopes starting from ₹35,000 with 100% client credential ownership and a strict 2x return screen.

---

## Design Language & Aesthetic

Inspired by the dark, high-contrast, modern architectural aesthetic of [growwwtech.com](https://growwwtech.com/):

- **Palette:** Deep midnight emerald forest (`#021a0d` page, `#032612` card surfaces, `#064024` soft tone) paired with electric lime (`#baf915` CTA fill, `#021a0d` ink).
- **Typography:** Geometric modernism with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, bold single-word display headline (`COVERDO` at 96px, 0 line wraps), and clean 18px body measure.
- **Visual Grounding:** Real archival freight logistics photography and loop footage. Zero resting shadows, zero 1px hairlines, and zero generic icons.

---

## Features & Architecture

- **Split Hero Card:** Brand statement, live operations badge (`● Active Operations · 30m SLA`), and authentic freight depot imagery.
- **Operational Trust Strip:** 4 core commitments (2x Hurdle, 100% Client Ownership, 30-Min SLA Review, 6 Receipts).
- **Volume Calibration ("Pick where you are today"):** Stage selector cards for Early D2C (500–2k/mo), Scaling D2C (2k–10k/mo), and High-Volume Enterprise (10k+/mo).
- **Unowned Operational Gaps:** 12-column asymmetric layout breaking down delivery exceptions, questionable COD orders, checkout objections, split shipments, and discrepant reporting.
- **Interactive Unit Economics Calculator:** Real-time client-side calculation computing break-even units, monthly expense amortization, 2x hurdle target, net bottom-line contribution, and conservative feasibility warnings.
- **Standalone Solutions Catalog:** Bounded scopes with transparent fixed pricing (₹35k–₹90k).
- **Tested Operational Dossiers:** Verified proof patterns (+8.2% NDR recovery, -18.4% RTO freight reduction).
- **Freight Logistics Gallery:** High-resolution authentic archival logistics photography.
- **Six Milestone Receipts:** Phased delivery timeline with concrete proof deliverables before each milestone.
- **Mutual Qualification Matrix:** Transparent "Fit" vs "Pass" criteria.
- **Semantic FAQ Accordion:** Native `<details>` / `<summary>` structure.
- **Conversion Loop Form:** 30-minute review intake card with instant client-side feedback.
