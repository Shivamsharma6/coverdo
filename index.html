Straight answers, with numbers

Brutally honest qualifications. We state what we do not do before discussing what we can deliver.

Why not native tools? +
If your courier or CRM provider already resolves delivery exceptions end-to-end and carrier reattempts are reliably acknowledged, you do not need us. Most stores find that customer WhatsApp responses stall in unmonitored queues and courier cutoffs expire before anyone acts. Coverdo builds and monitors the operational queue between them.
Performance guarantees? +
Pure contingency arrangements create perverse incentives to claim credit for organic customer reattempts and dispute settlements. We charge transparent fixed fees and enforce an upfront 2x benefit-to-cost hurdle. If your volume cannot conservatively deliver double our fee, we state it in our 30-minute evaluation and refuse the engagement.
Why not an internal hire? +
An internal hire requires 3 months of onboarding, costs ₹40,000–₹70,000 monthly in fixed overhead, and rarely possesses cross-functional technical skills across webhook logic, courier escalation channels, and margin accounting. Coverdo delivers tested battle-proven integrations live within 2 to 4 weeks.
Who owns the accounts? +
You retain 100% credential and data ownership. All integrations, webhook endpoints, and platform licenses are created within your company accounts. You can revoke our access or operate the runbooks independently at any time.
How fast is deployment? +
A standard standalone scope (such as Delivery Exception Resolution or Pre-Dispatch COD Confirmation) is deployed within 10 to 15 working days following Milestone 1 audit approval, including testing under a monitored live boundary.
