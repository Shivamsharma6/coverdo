07 / BEFORE WE START

Clear answers.
Fewer loose ends.

Do we need a certain order volume?+

Fit depends on the service, the problem, your economics and the data available. Delivery operations need enough actionable volume to justify the cost. Creative, ads and landing pages are evaluated on their own scope; there is no blanket COD or monthly-order threshold.

Can we buy just one service?+

Yes. Each service has a bounded first scope. If another dependency is necessary, it is identified and priced before agreement. You do not have to buy a bundle or an ongoing retainer.

What does the price include?+

The catalogue shows indicative fees for the described first scope. Your proposal fixes setup, recurring responsibilities, external costs, taxes, term, payment milestones and exclusions. Software, ad spend, message/call usage and unusual migration costs are extra unless explicitly included.

Who owns the accounts and the work?+

Your business retains its accounts, credentials and data. The proposal specifies access, delivered assets, documentation, third-party licences and handover responsibilities. Support coverage and incident ownership are agreed explicitly.

What if our current software already solves this?+

We review existing capabilities first. If configuration or your provider can close the gap adequately, that may be the right route. An engagement needs a useful remaining implementation, integration or operating responsibility.

Do you guarantee delivery rates or ROAS?+

We agree deliverables, acceptance evidence and a measurement plan. Courier execution, customer choices, product economics and media conditions affect commercial outcomes. We assess results against the agreed baseline and completed business outcomes.
